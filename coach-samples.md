# Ask the coach samples

Move notes are hand-written, one for every ply of the 10 Scotch Gambit lines. The mock coach speaks that note. A stored computer check can still sit in the prompt as a private balance hint, with no decimals and no permission to mention it. If an answer contains a score, the word engine, or Stockfish, the server asks once more and then drops the bad sentences. What is left, if anything, is the written note.

These are Practice notes for Line 1. The same words are the fallback when the gateway is off.

## Line 1, after 1.e4

> e4 grabs a big share of the centre and opens lines for the bishop and queen.

## Line 1, after 2.Nf3

> Nf3 develops a knight towards the centre and attacks the pawn on e5.

## Line 1, after 3.d4

> d4 strikes at the centre straight away. This is the Scotch.

## Line 1, after 4.Bc4

> Bc4 aims at f7, Black's weak spot.

The intro on that line is: "This is Line 1, one of 10 lines from Opening Lab. The main 5.c3 gambit: meet Bb4+ with Bd2, win the pawn back by exd5, and castle. Tap Practice when you want to play the moves."

In Test, a note talks about the move just played. It does not name the next book move.
